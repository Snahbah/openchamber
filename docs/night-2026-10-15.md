# OpenChamber hardening — night of 2026-10-15

## What was broken (three compounding defects)

1. **No default token budget.** `parseGoalMetadata` (session-goal/runtime.js L184)
   defaulted `tokenBudget` to `null`, so a goal with no explicit budget had no hard
   cap — only the 20-turn auto-continuation limit. Compaction then skipped the
   audit and continued unconditionally (L780), and the summarisation call reported
   0 tokens (L679-685), so any budget undercounted. Net effect: a goal could drive
   through compaction after compaction up to 20 turns of full cloud-model calls.
2. **`sessionGoalRuntime.stop()` was never called.** No HTTP route, no call site, no
   in-flight interruption.
3. **Routing fallback fell through on quota exhaustion.** A 429 from the classifier
   still routed to the fallback model and spent.

Impact: a week of Claude Max and 44% of Gemini Ultra weekly quota consumed in two
5-hour windows.

## What was patched (install, `%APPDATA%\npm\node_modules\@openchamber\web`)

- `server/lib/session-goal/runtime.js`: mandatory 200,000 default budget (L184);
  compaction summary audited (not skipped); summarisation cost estimated from the
  summary text.
- `server/lib/routing/runtime.js`: quota-exhausted (429/402/quota message) → refuse
  (throw 429); every fallback logged `{event:"routing.fallback", from, to, reason,
  sessionId, timestamp}` and counted (`fallbacks_total`, `fallbacks_by_reason`).
- `server/index.js`: `POST /api/goals/stop-all` kill switch + `listActiveGoalSessions`
  seam + fallback counter surfaced on `/health`.
- Launcher `~/.config/openchamber/bin/OpenChamber.ps1`: pre-flight block kills any
  stale process on port 3000 before starting (fixes the S4U orphan that
  `Stop-ScheduledTask` left behind).
